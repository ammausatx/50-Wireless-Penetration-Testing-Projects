## Project walkthrough standard

Each project includes objectives, scope, prerequisites, a step-by-step workflow, expected observations, risk analysis, remediation, validation, and evidence/reporting guidance. Simulated screenshots are training illustrations, not genuine test evidence.

## Safety and authorization

- Obtain written authorization and define SSIDs, BSSIDs, locations, dates, and permitted methods before testing.
- Use an isolated lab AP and synthetic test clients/credentials.
- Do not capture, crack, disrupt, impersonate, or access third-party networks or users.
- Do not deploy credential-harvesting portals or conduct deauthentication against real users.
- Potentially disruptive techniques are discussed only as defensive configuration/detection validation; no operational attack instructions are provided.
- Follow local radio-frequency regulations and vendor guidance.
